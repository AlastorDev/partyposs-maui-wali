Preserve the blue pointed cupcake-pattern birthday party hat with yellow pompom and the blue birthday rosette/ribbon worn on its chest. Full body, all hat, tail and feet fully visible, centered in portrait framing with comfortable transparent margin on all sides. This must be a genuinely transparent background with real alpha, including the spaces around the curled tail and fine fur edges. No scenery, no ground plane, no drop shadow, no cast shadow, no UI, no text, no extra objects. Do not place the creature on a white, black, solid-color or checkerboard backdrop; return actual alpha transparency.

No floor, no pedestal, no drop or cast shadows, no scene, no other objects, no numerals, no letters, no text, no UI, no crop, no divider, no checkerboard rendered into pixels.
